To configure SMTP for your local environment, you have two options depending on what you want to achieve. Since you are testing on localhost, the easiest and safest way to test emails without actually sending them to real addresses is by using an email testing tool like Mailtrap, or you can use your Gmail account to send real emails.

Here is how you can set it up directly from your application's UI:

Step 1: Open the Settings
In your application, navigate to the Settings page from the sidebar.
Under the Global Project section, click on SMTP Server (or Email settings).
Step 2: Choose your Provider & Enter Details
Option A: Use Mailtrap (Recommended for Local Testing)
Mailtrap catches all outgoing emails from your localhost so you can view them safely in a fake inbox.

Go to Mailtrap.io and create a free account.
Navigate to Email Testing > Inboxes > My Inbox > Show Credentials.
Enter the details in your app's Settings:
SMTP Host: sandbox.smtp.mailtrap.io
SMTP Port: 2525 (or 587)
SMTP Username: (copy from Mailtrap)
SMTP Password: (copy from Mailtrap)
Encryption: TLS
Option B: Use Gmail (To send real emails)
If you want to send actual emails to your inbox from localhost:

Go to your Google Account Settings -> Security.
Enable 2-Step Verification (required).
Search for App Passwords and generate a new password (name it something like "Local App").
Enter the details in your app's Settings:
SMTP Host: smtp.gmail.com
SMTP Port: 587
SMTP Username: your-email@gmail.com
SMTP Password: (The 16-character App Password you just generated)
Encryption: TLS
Once you save these settings, your application will pull them directly from the database, and the new Share Documents via Email feature will start working instantly!
